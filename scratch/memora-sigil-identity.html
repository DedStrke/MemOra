02 · Construction

Icon anatomy

Three strokes, one silhouette. Geass supplies the geometry and the feeling - angular activation, a mark that reads as command rather than decoration - never its eye.

Command mark · the blade

The central angular form - focus and direction. Its diagonal wing is the identity's main axis, echoed in the wordmark's own M cut.

Activation sweep · the cradle arc

Momentum and flow. It deliberately levitates the blade rather than touching it - the mark reads as hovering, activated, not resting.

Rising terminal · the shard

Ambition and progression. Extends on hover, and is echoed directly by the small shard cut into the wordmark's "o".

Level-up fragment · hidden by default

Achievement. Opacity zero at rest - only an XP gain or a level up releases it, briefly, before it dissolves.

M

A slight angular cut on the left stroke, echoing the blade's diagonal. Still unmistakably a modern M.

A ring broken by one angular cut, with a small detached shard. Reads instantly as an "o" - the open gap is what keeps it from ever reading as an eye.

a

A subtle upward terminal on the tail, the wordmark's quiet nod to progression - never dramatic, never an anime title font.

03 · Motion

Animation storyboard

One icon, five states - no new shapes are drawn for any of them. Idle is looping now; hover it directly; click the buttons to fire XP gain and level up.

01

Idle

3.4s loop

Blade drifts half a pixel, sweep tilts a degree. Calm enough for a navbar forever.

02

Hover / focus

180-240ms

Blade lifts, sweep tightens, terminal extends with a small overshoot. Hover this card.

03

Click

250-350ms

Compresses, twists a few degrees, snaps back.

04

XP gain

350-450ms

Terminal extends, one fragment detaches upward and dissolves. No stars, no confetti.

05

Level up

650-850ms

Charge, burst, settle with a small bounce. The signature moment.

Rules

Single colour only - currentColor, never a baked-in hex.
No stars, sparkles, lightning, magic circles, mascots.
No eyes, pupils, irises, or watching motifs - none, ever.
Silhouette must hold at 16px - test before shipping any change.
Same four paths everywhere: nav, favicon, hero, every state.
Wordmark stays "Memora" - the "o" is the only customised letter.
